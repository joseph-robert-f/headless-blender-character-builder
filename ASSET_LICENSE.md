# Sample asset license policy

No sample model, render, animation, reference image, or other artwork is included in the initial planning commit.

Unless a per-file manifest says otherwise, future original sample assets created specifically for this project will be dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each published sample should record its creator, source, license, modifications, and checksum.

The GPL license for project source code does not automatically apply to artwork, user inputs, or normal generated outputs. Likewise, this policy does not grant rights to third-party names, characters, brands, references, or likenesses. Users remain responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights to submitted materials and generated uses.
