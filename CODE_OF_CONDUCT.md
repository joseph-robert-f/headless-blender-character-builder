# Code of conduct

We want project spaces to be constructive, technically rigorous, and welcoming.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, accept good-faith correction, protect private information, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption.

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, project chat, events, and public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when behavior undermines a safe and productive community.

Report sensitive conduct concerns through the private route described in `SECURITY.md`. Do not include personal or confidential details in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports as privately and fairly as practical.
