# `facet-bot` Example Brief

`facet-bot` is an original geometric desk-toy robot designed to prove the bounded character generator without relying on a franchise, likeness, uploaded asset, font, texture, or network resource.

The default version has:

- a rounded-cube head with two inset circular eyes;
- a compact capsule-like torso;
- stout cylindrical arms and legs with spherical joints;
- a stable circular pedestal integrated into the printable shell;
- a warm orange and cream palette for presentation renders;
- a neutral standing pose and friendly proportions;
- no text, logo, trademark, or external reference.

The form must remain recognizable from front, side, and back views and must compile entirely from native Blender mesh primitives. The printable STL is one connected watertight positive-volume shell. Nominal target height is 95 mm, minimum walls are 1.2 mm, and freestanding features or connections are at least 2.0 mm.

The second required fixture should use the same generator but materially change style, proportions, palette, pose/component selection, topology counts, bounds, and structural fingerprint.
